# Product Marketing Assignment — Submission Note

**Chosen comparison:** SurveySparrow vs SurveyMonkey

I chose the SurveySparrow vs SurveyMonkey comparison because it is a familiar category with a clear product-marketing opportunity: the original comparison is largely feature-led, while a buyer usually needs to understand the workflow and outcome behind those features.

I rebuilt the page around a new argument: **don’t pick a survey tool; pick a feedback workflow.** Instead of recreating a feature checklist, the page asks three decision questions — will people answer, how deep does the research need to go, and what happens after the response? The comparison then maps SurveySparrow and SurveyMonkey against those jobs.

I kept out long product descriptions, exhaustive feature lists, generic “winner” language, and unsupported claims. I also avoided pretending that one product is universally better. The page makes the trade-off explicit: SurveySparrow is positioned around conversational feedback and action-oriented workflows, while SurveyMonkey is positioned around research breadth, advanced survey tooling, and ecosystem depth.

The visual system was rebuilt from scratch: a dark-green editorial palette, oversized typography, decision cards, a compact comparison table, and use-case sections designed for scanning. The CTA is intentionally tied to the core product promise rather than simply repeating “Book a demo.”

Current official product/pricing pages were checked while building the comparison. Pricing is shown as the currently listed annual-billed starting points and should be rechecked immediately before submission because plans can change.

**How to use:** open `index.html` locally to review the finished page. For the required live URL, deploy this file/folder to Netlify, Vercel, GitHub Pages, or another static host and submit that public URL.
